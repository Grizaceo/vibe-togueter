Playing and joining the conversation

The server receives your chosen browser nickname, game controls and chat text. Twitch supplies channel usernames and chat messages. Accepted messages and game actions can appear to other players, the studio and the OBS overlay. The recent feed holds up to 60 entries in server memory. Game-specific state may retain names and actions until that game resets or the server stops. Your browser remembers its nickname in localStorage under va-name; clearing site data removes that copy.

A connection gets a random voting identity. Proposal rounds retain author names, proposal text, source, timestamps, decisions, hashed actor identifiers and ballots on the operator's disk. A second idea journal also retains submitted ideas. Public rounds show authors and vote totals. A hash is pseudonymous data, not a promise of anonymity.

Contributing and building

Creator submissions include display names, portfolio/social links, resource addresses, descriptions, licenses and fingerprints. Pending submissions are visible to the curator; accepted resources and their credit links become public. Review decisions and version usage are retained. Do not submit private contact information or secrets in public fields.

When an operator initiates construction, selected idea text and the approved resource metadata may be sent to the chosen coding service and build VM. Metadata can include author names, attribution and public resource links. Isolated testing can receive approved asset bytes. Provider retention depends on that service and account. The software does not implement an artwork-training service; this notice does not promise that all providers have identical retention or training settings. Build/quarantine records retain candidate source, reports, hashes, approvals and job metadata until the operator removes them. Some reviewed source and evidence may be included in a repository or recording.

Connected services and event records

Twitch receives chat replies and authentication requests. The operator's OAuth credentials are kept in a restricted local configuration file; they are not sent to players or game guests. Signed EventSub notifications may contain usernames and reward details and can trigger public game effects. Recent delivery identifiers and times are journaled to deduplicate retries. Simulated event-credit accounting is disabled in the submission setup. An operator who explicitly enables legacy fixtures may persist event usernames, amounts and sources in the demo ledger. This is not a payment or donation service.

Your IP and connection metadata necessarily reach the server and any hosting or tunnel provider. This application does not maintain an IP access-log feature, but infrastructure providers can keep their own logs. External game links, creator websites, Twitch and coding/hosting providers operate under their own policies. The shipped remote-game catalog is empty. Enabling a remote runtime can send it player names and controls.

Retention, recording and choices

Persistent round, idea, creator, ledger, delivery and build records do not have automatic expiry or a user-facing deletion tool. They remain until the operator deletes them, subject to backups and any evidence already shared. Server logs can include operational errors and game logs. A stream or video may preserve names, chat and gameplay after the live state ends; ask the operator about recordings before participating.

Use a nickname you are comfortable showing publicly. Avoid putting personal or sensitive data into proposals or chat. Close the page to disconnect and clear browser site data to remove the saved nickname. Contact the operator above to request access, correction or deletion and identify the nickname, resource or round involved. Applicable privacy rights depend on your location. The operator must establish a working contact and a retention/deletion procedure before public operation.

No advertising SDK, analytics tracker or application cookie is included. This software does not sell personal data or implement age verification. Twitch participation remains subject to Twitch's account rules. Administrative keys are held in memory by the control pages and clear on reload or locking; never place them in a public link.
